🪙 Coin Scout V2 — Visual Assist

Photo-assisted, local-first screening. No OpenAI/API key required.
Important

This version uses the camera for actual visual evidence, but it will not invent a year or mint mark when the photo is unclear. OCR is a helper, not the final authority.

The goal is to get real visual recognition working incrementally instead of pretending the photo is being analyzed when it isn't.
