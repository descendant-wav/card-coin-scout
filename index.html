🚦 V9 rules
  • COMMON = bulk-pile move-on result.
  • MELT = intrinsic silver value detected; keep it.
  • MELT+ = silver plus a collector-value warning; don't price it as scrap automatically.
  • CHECK = something needs a closer look, weight test, variety check, or exact issue identification.
  • HOT = key date, major variety, major error possibility, or old/rare issue; set it aside.
Important: the scanner is a triage tool, not an authentication service. If a coin hits a major key date or famous error, preserve it and verify it before selling.
