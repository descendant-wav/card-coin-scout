V5 is deliberately honest.
  • It analyzes the actual photo, not just your typed fields.
  • It runs the visual model on the whole image and a tighter coin crop, and averages front/back evidence when both are supplied.
  • It treats OCR as supporting evidence only. A bad OCR read cannot override the visual evidence.
  • It never invents an exact year or mint. If the photo cannot prove it, the app says so.
The first visual run downloads the browser model and may take a while. It is cached afterward.
