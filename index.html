Why V4 is different
  • The previous model was generic CLIP. V4 uses the creator's Coin-CLIP retrieval system, which was fine-tuned on more than 340,000 coin images specifically for coin image retrieval.
  • The official Coin-CLIP demo compares your image against a large U.S.-coin image database and returns visually similar coin images.
  • Coin-CLIP is a retrieval model, not a magic year-reading classifier. The retrieved images are evidence, not a guaranteed exact date.
  • No OpenAI key or OpenAI tokens are involved. The Coin-CLIP computation happens on the public Hugging Face demo when you press Analyze.
